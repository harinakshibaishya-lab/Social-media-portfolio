Case Study 01

Brand Refresh: Making a Modern Brand More Social

Brand: Vela (Fictional Lifestyle / Wellness Brand)
Speculative Brand Project
Speculative Brand Project: Created to demonstrate strategic brand repositioning, editorial consistency, and social-first asset creation for a contemporary wellness brand.

The Challenge

Vela had high-quality, clean wellness formulations but struggled with inconsistent social content, disjointed visual aesthetics, and zero clear content pillars.

  • Posts were published erratically without thematic consistency

Brand: Vela • Channel Launch Strategy
Campaign Concept / Speculative Work
Campaign Concept / Speculative Work: A multi-week activation model created to demonstrate full-funnel campaign planning, community engagement rituals, and multi-format content distribution.

Campaign Goal & Audience

Goal: Build organic brand awareness, foster active community participation, and drive high-intent email newsletter opt-ins through a 30-day challenge.

Case Study 03

Social Media Audit: What I Would Improve

Reference Brand: Glossier (Independent External Analysis)
Independent Portfolio Analysis
Independent Portfolio Analysis: This analysis was prepared as an independent creative exercise for portfolio evaluation. Harinakshi Baishya is not affiliated with or endorsed by Glossier.

Current Observations

Glossier retains iconic aesthetic recognition and strong brand nostalgia, but its recent feed exhibits high reliance on polished product packshots with declining organic comments.

  • Audience: Highly engaged in DMs and TikTok comments, but feed carousels lack save-worthy educational utility.
  • Formats: Over-indexing on static product images; short-form video could lean more into honest problem-solution skin tutorials.

Content Gaps & Opportunities

  • Gap: Missing direct ingredient transparency breakdowns that younger skincare-savvy shoppers demand.
  • Opportunity: Introduce "The Honest Routine" series featuring real skin textures in natural daylight without studio lighting filters.
  • Engagement Loop: Replace broadcast captions with interactive community questions regarding discontinued shades and everyday routines.

What I Look For In The Data

Which content earns saves?
High saves indicate practical utility. Step-by-step frameworks and curated resources earn 4.2x more saves than generic lifestyle imagery.
Which formats generate shares?
Relatable situational humor and validating quotes drive peer forwards, introducing the brand to lookalike audiences without ad spend.
Which hooks improve retention?
Direct problem statements in the first 2 seconds reduce drop-off by over 36% compared to generic introductory greetings.
Which topics create conversations?
Contrarian takes on industry dogmas ("Why posting daily is counterproductive") yield the deepest, highest-quality comment threads.

Transformation Case Study

From Random Posts to a Content System

How structuring consistent editorial pillars transforms chaotic social feeds into predictable brand assets.

BEFORE: The Guesswork Cycle

  • Random Topics: Posting whatever idea emerged 30 minutes before publishing.
  • Inconsistent Formats: Unpredictable mix of styles with no visual recognizable rhythm.
  • Unclear Audience: Trying to appeal to everyone and resonating with nobody.
  • Weak CTAs: Vague sign-offs like "let us know" resulting in zero comment activity.
  • Creator Burnout: Daily panic over what to post next with no editorial reserve.

AFTER: The Content Engine

  • Defined Audience: Hyperspecific persona with addressed pain points and language.
  • 5 Content Pillars: Reliable thematic pillars balancing education, lifestyle, and product.
  • Recurring Formats: Established weekly franchises (Sunday Reset, Tuesday Breakdown).
  • Clear, High-Intent CTAs: Specific requests for saves, shares, or comment votes.
  • Monthly Roadmap: 30-day proactive batching reducing stress and boosting quality.
